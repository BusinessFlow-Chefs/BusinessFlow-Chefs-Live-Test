# BusinessFlow Chefs — Charter Design Lock

Status: ACTIVE APPROVED DIRECTION / CONTENDER VISUALS
Date: 2026-09-08

## Core flow
The Charter flow must be reduced to four screens, not five:
1. Charter Home
2. New Charter — combines the previous New Charter + Additional Details screens
3. Guests & Preferences
4. Charter Summary

Do not reintroduce a separate Additional Details page unless explicitly approved later.

## 1. Charter Home
- Keep premium yacht / deep navy / platinum / metallic-gold visual family.
- Primary action: `+ New Charter`.
- Remove `Favourites`, `Upcoming`, and `Previous` tab structure.
- Do NOT use a mini yearly dot-calendar as the main yearly overview.
- Show the year as a clear chronological list/database of charters, with each charter visibly showing its dates.
- Previous charters act as a reusable database and retain all stored charter information, including guests, allergies, dietary needs, food preferences, menus, notes, destinations/stops, service structure and other relevant details.
- Search/filter for historic charters remains useful.

## 2. New Charter — single compact setup page
This page absorbs the former Additional Details page to save a step.
Must include, in one screen/expandable sections:
- S.A.G.E. voice entry option.
- Start and end dates.
- Departure with accurate live type-ahead location search; suggestions narrow with every letter entered.
- Main destination with the same accurate live type-ahead behaviour.
- Additional stop-off points / multiple destinations, addable and reorderable.
- Number of guests.
- Guest allergies / special dietary requirements at charter level, with full per-guest detail completed/edited in Guests & Preferences.
- Number of services per day.
- Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner are core defaults; Snacks / Extras optional.
- Budget optional.
- If no budget is entered, S.A.G.E. should offer/recommend an appropriate budget based on guests, duration, route, service level, menu/theme choices and preferences rather than blocking creation.
- Vessel optional.
- Service times / use standard times option.
- Special requests / notes.
- Themed nights selector.

## Themed nights
- Must support a large/vast theme library, not a short token list.
- Themes include at minimum: Mediterranean, Fine Dining, Italian, French Riviera, Spanish Tapas & Paella, Provençal, Greek, Seafood, BBQ & Grill, Japanese, Sushi & Sashimi, Thai, Indian, Middle Eastern Mezze, Mexican, Caribbean, Steakhouse, Surf & Turf, Plant-Based, Wellness & Clean Eating, Farm-to-Table, Beach Club, Street Food, Family Comfort, Celebration, Romantic Dinner, Chef’s Tasting Menu, Asian Fusion, Local Port, Chef’s Choice.
- Chef can apply a theme to:
  - a specific day/date, OR
  - the full charter.
- Multiple themed nights across different days must be supported.
- Theme selection must not force fish/seafood duplication across starter/main; existing meal-balance rules still apply.

## 3. Guests & Preferences
- Per-guest allergies, dietary needs, preferences, dislikes, special occasions and notes.
- Ability to reuse/import guest lists and Guest Memory data from previous charters.

## 4. Charter Summary
- Show dates, route including stop-off count/itinerary, guest count, services/day, budget (or S.A.G.E. recommended budget status), allergies/dietary flags, service structure and themes.
- Primary action: `Continue to Guests` or next logical workflow step.
- Secondary action: `Skip to Home` must be clearly visible.
- Summary remains editable.

## S.A.G.E.
Working name approved for current design use:
S.A.G.E. = Service Assistant / AI-Powered Galley Expert.
Use as the chef assistant for voice entry and intelligent recommendations.

## Yacht imagery
- Use different premium yacht imagery across pages where it helps keep the app fresh, while preserving a coherent visual family.
- Do not let changing yacht imagery change navigation, layout, typography, interaction patterns or the established navy/platinum/metallic-gold design system.
