# BusinessFlow Chefs — Charter Design Checkpoint

Status: APPROVED DIRECTION; visual implementation to be reviewed before final lock.

This checkpoint records Darren's approved Charter simplification direction so it cannot be lost or reinterpreted.

## Visual direction to preserve
- Keep the premium BusinessFlow Chefs visual family: luxury yacht photography, deep navy, platinum and metallic-gold accents.
- Keep BusinessFlow Chefs branding and the `Charter` page title.
- Reduce the Charter hero height by about 20% compared with the previous busy contender.
- Remove the large central glowing `Charter` orb.
- Remove extra internal-app marketing clutter such as `Plan. Provision. Deliver.`, `Exceptional cuisine at sea`, and the handwritten slogan.
- Keep only one short supporting line under the Charter title, e.g. `Plan your charter`.

## Charter page information hierarchy
Keep one clean charter summary card showing only:
- Charter name
- Dates
- Route
- Guests
- Budget

Below the summary, show only three clean expandable rows:
1. `Charter Details`
2. `Service Structure`
3. `Guests`

Do not expose every editable field at once on the main Charter screen.

## Expandable Charter Details behavior
When `Charter Details` is opened, it must expose the actual editable charter fields, including:
- Charter name
- Vessel
- Departure
- Destination
- Accurate location type-ahead/search for departure and destination
- Start date
- End date
- Auto-calculated number of days where applicable
- Guest count
- Budget
- Overlap warning against existing charters
- Cancel charter control
- Other required charter setup fields already approved elsewhere

When the details panel is closed, the clean summary card must update automatically from the edited values.

## Service structure
- Core services are Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.
- Snacks / Extras are optional, not mandatory.
- Service Structure is expandable rather than fully exposed on the main Charter screen.

## Navigation / CTA
- Keep the large metallic-gold `Continue to Guests` button.
- Keep the five-position bottom navigation concept.
- Preserve the enlarged metallic-gold centre `Chef Mode` button.
- Do not introduce extra bottom-navigation items.

## Product intent
The Charter screen should feel calm and simple: very little visible at once, while no required functionality is removed. The chef should be able to review the charter at a glance, open only the section they need to edit, then continue naturally into Guests.
