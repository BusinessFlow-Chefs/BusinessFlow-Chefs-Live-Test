# BusinessFlow Chefs — Charter Design Checkpoint

Status: APPROVED DIRECTION; visual implementation to be reviewed before final lock.

This checkpoint records Darren's approved Charter simplification direction so it cannot be lost or reinterpreted.

## Visual direction to preserve
- Keep the premium BusinessFlow Chefs visual family: luxury yacht photography, deep navy, platinum and metallic-gold accents.
- Keep BusinessFlow Chefs branding and the `Charter` page title.
- Reduce the Charter hero height compared with the earlier busy contender.
- Remove the large central glowing `Charter` orb.
- Remove extra internal-app marketing clutter.
- Remove `Welcome back, Chef` from Charter; that greeting belongs on Home only.
- Keep the Charter page calm and sparse rather than showing everything at once.

## S.A.G.E. presence
- S.A.G.E. must have a more central, visible role on Charter because it is a unique BusinessFlow feature.
- S.A.G.E. = Service Assistant / AI-Powered Galley Expert; exact expansion wording remains subject to final naming choice.
- Charter Home should give S.A.G.E. a prominent voice/create entry point without replacing the manual `New Charter` action.

## Charter Home
- Keep a clear `New Charter` action.
- `2026 Booking Overview` and `Charters for 2026` are duplicate concepts and should NOT appear as separate sections.
- Merge them into one single collapsed dropdown, suggested label: `Charter Database 2026`.
- The collapsed row may show a concise summary such as `12 charters | 86 guest days`.
- All dropdown / expandable sections start CLOSED by default.
- When expanded, saved charters are listed in date order for the year.
- Opening a saved charter shows the complete saved record: route and stops, dates, vessel, guests, allergies/dietary needs, food preferences, services, budget, theme, notes and any other stored charter information.

## New Charter flow
- Keep the journey in logical order: Departure -> optional stop-off point(s) -> Final Destination -> optional Return Journey.
- Accurate location type-ahead must narrow suggestions with each entered letter.
- Additional Details is not a separate page; useful fields are folded into New Charter.
- Capture dates, guest count, services/day, journey, special requests/allergies and optional budget while keeping the page compact.
- If no budget is supplied, S.A.G.E. should suggest a recommended budget rather than blocking progress.
- Preferred themed night belongs on New Charter as a compact optional dropdown.
- Theme selector is collapsed by default; after choosing a theme, show `Apply to: Full charter / Specific day(s)`.
- Theme library should be extensive, including Mediterranean, Italian, Greek, French Riviera, Spanish, Japanese, Thai, Indian, Middle Eastern, Mexican, Caribbean, Steakhouse, Surf & Turf, Plant-Based, Wellness, Farm-to-Table, Beach Club, Street Food, Family Comfort, Celebration, Romantic, Chef's Tasting, Local Port, Chef's Choice and more.

## Guests
- Individual guest allergies, dietary needs and preferences must be captured and saved.
- Previous charter data should support recalling guest lists/preferences through Guest Memory.

## Service structure
- Core services are Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.
- Snacks / Extras are optional, not mandatory.

## Navigation / CTA
- Preserve the five-position premium bottom navigation concept.
- Preserve the enlarged metallic-gold centre `Chef Mode` button.
- Do not introduce extra bottom-navigation items without approval.
- Confirmation flow includes a secondary `Skip to Home` option.

## Product intent
The Charter area should feel calm and simple: very little visible at once, while no required functionality is removed. The chef opens only what they need, S.A.G.E. stays visibly available, and the charter database becomes one clear source of past and future charter history rather than duplicated panels.
