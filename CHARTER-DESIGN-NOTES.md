# BusinessFlow Chefs — Charter Design Notes

## Status
**Current Charter contender is NOT approved.**

## User feedback — 6 September 2026
- At a glance the Charter screen contains too much information.
- The screen feels too busy.
- Future Charter iterations must reduce visual density and prioritise essential setup information.

## Simplification direction
Preserve the BusinessFlow Chefs premium visual language (deep navy, platinum, metallic gold, yacht imagery, consistent bottom navigation) while simplifying the Charter task.

### Keep immediately visible
- Charter title / concise hero
- Charter Name
- Departure
- Destination
- Start Date
- End Date
- Guests
- Budget
- Core services: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
- Primary `Create Charter` action

### Reduce / move out of the first glance
- Remove duplicate marketing/tagline copy from the working form.
- Reduce the large central Charter orb; it is decorative duplication on a data-entry screen.
- Move Vessel into `More details` unless it is required for the current charter workflow.
- Remove descriptive copy under each service option.
- Snacks / Extras must remain optional and must NOT appear preselected as a core service.
- Do not show a separate `Continue to Guests` action until after the charter has been created/saved.
- Keep fields slim, uniform and easy to scan vertically.

### Design objective
A chef should understand and complete the Charter screen in seconds without scanning a promotional page. The page should feel like a premium operational form, not a feature catalogue.
