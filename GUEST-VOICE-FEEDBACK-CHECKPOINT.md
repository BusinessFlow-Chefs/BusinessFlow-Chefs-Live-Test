# BusinessFlow Chefs — Guest Voice Feedback Checkpoint

Status: APPROVED FEATURE DIRECTION

## Guest Feedback
- Guest feedback must feed into Guest Memory after meals/services and across future charters.
- Chef can record simple structured feedback such as: loved it / liked it / disliked it / never again / favourite, with optional notes.
- Feedback should influence future menu generation and recommendations for that guest.

## Voice Feedback to S.A.G.E.
- Guests should have an option to give spoken feedback directly to S.A.G.E.
- The flow should be lightweight and guest-friendly, not expose operational chef controls.
- S.A.G.E. should capture the spoken feedback, transcribe/summarise it, associate it with the correct guest, meal/service and charter, and store it in Guest Memory.
- Examples: favourite dish, disliked ingredient, preferred spice level, portion-size comments, breakfast preferences, dessert preferences, service comments, future requests.
- S.A.G.E. should confirm the captured meaning before permanently storing ambiguous or sensitive preference/allergy changes.
- Allergy/safety-critical statements from guests must not silently overwrite confirmed allergy records; they should be flagged for chef confirmation.
- Feedback should be available to the chef later as concise Guest Memory insights and should influence future menu generation without forcing repetitive menu choices.

## Product intent
Guest voice feedback should make BusinessFlow feel personal and intelligent while keeping chef workflow fast and guest interaction simple.
